Beginner path — zero to pro in 7 steps

1. Challenge. $10K sim, make $500, lose >5% in a day or >10% total and you are out. Wins above $150 count only partially.
2. Ideas. Break-retest, head-and-shoulders, engulfing candle, support/resistance zone, trend average, 1–2 week hold, 1.5% risk.
3. Rule-explicit. Every idea becomes a yes/no test on candles/zones/averages. Vague is untestable.
4. Backtest. Replay bar by bar with costs and no future-peek. Score win rate, profit factor, expectancy, streaks, dips.
5. Shuffle. Same trades, 5,000 orders — how often do you hit target before ruin? Here: 2%.
6. Read the null. 40 trades in 6.7 years ≈ 6/year; challenges need 3–5/week. Daily bars starve the intraday system.
7. Next falsifiable step. Rebuild on 4-hour bars + session filter + structure stops, pre-register, demand 100–300 trades.
